Research Area

Agentic AI

Autonomous Systems

Containerized Microservices

Container orchestration, zero-trust microservice architectures, and secure, scalable service deployment.

Software Defined Wireless Sensor Networks, Software Defined Internet of Vehicles, and Security, Cloud/Fog Computing, etc.

Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)

Intelligent Traffic Light Controllers, Traffic Flow Prediction, Autonomous Vehicles, Applied Machine Learning, Battery Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure Optimisations, etc.
