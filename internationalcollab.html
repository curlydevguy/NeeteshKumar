Dr. Azim Eskandarian - professor and head of the Department of Mechanical Engineering in the College of Engineering at Virginia Tech, has been named the Nicholas and Rebecca Des Champs Chair in Mechanical Engineering by the Virginia Tech Board of Visitors. He is an internationally known researcher in the field of Autonomous vehicles and vehicle safety. Please visit his site and LinkedIn profile here.
Dr. Devki Nandan Jha - A Postdoctoral Research Associate/Research Software Engineer working on the Trusted Public Cloud project. He completed his PhD from the School of Computing, Newcastle University, and was also a visiting researcher at A*STAR Singapore. Please visit his site.
